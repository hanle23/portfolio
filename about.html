About Me!

It's nice of you to checking my profile out!

My name is Han, I graduated from York University in 2023 and has been working as a Software Engineer till now.

I started off working in the website space, where I would work on building new features, optimizing efficiently through selecting a suitable data structure or adjusting the logic.

All of this work is extremely interesting for me, as it feels like the contribution I made can be seen immediately on screen, the feeling of creating a new component and seeing it in front of you is just amazing. Though I acknowledge that you can achieve the same thing through game development.

At the same time, I do dream about doing something beyond that 2D plane, I do want to continue studying Computer Vision, maybe hardware, and be able to build and optimizing stuff in AR/VR environment.

I believe in the possibility of AR/VR be able to enhance human, our experience with computer. The ability to reach for sub-realisticly the world many people have built, to be able to see, to feel, to traverse the space as wide as a person imagination.

But in the meantime, I will continue to improve myself as an engineer, to be able to be knowledgable enough to do that.

Thank you for your time to read through my story.
