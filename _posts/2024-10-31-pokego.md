A Go client library for [PokeAPI](https://pokeapi.co/) v2 — typed endpoints for Pokémon data with a simple singleton client.

Inspired by [PokeAPI-GO](https://github.com/mtslzr/pokeapi-go), with coverage aligned to the official PokeAPI docs as of late 2024.

**Stack:** Go  

```go
client := pokego.NewClient()
pokemon, err := client.Pokemon("pikachu")
```
