A hackathon app focused on reducing food waste and supporting shelters.

Restaurants can list untouched meals available for pickup — including dietary and cultural restrictions — so shelters can claim them through the app. Built with mapping support via the Google Maps SDK.

**Stack:** Java, Google Maps SDK  
**Focus:** Zero hunger / sustainable communities (SDG-aligned)
